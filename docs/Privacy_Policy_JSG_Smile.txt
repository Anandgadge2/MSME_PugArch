1. INTRODUCTION 
JSG SMILE (www.jsgsmile.in) (“Portal”) is a procurement facilitation platform established 
under the guidance of the District Industries Centre (DIC), Jharsuguda, Government of 
Odisha, to promote business opportunities between Large Buyers and Micro, Small and 
Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) operating within Jharsuguda District. 
This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, stored, processed, 
disclosed, and protected when users access or use the Portal. 
By accessing, registering, or using the Portal, you consent to the practices described in this 
Privacy Policy. 
 
2. PURPOSE OF DATA COLLECTION 
The Portal collects information for the following purposes: 
• User registration and verification 
• MSME onboarding and validation 
• Buyer onboarding and validation 
• Facilitating procurement opportunities 
• Transaction management 
• Payment tracking and settlement facilitation 
• Communication and notifications 
• Compliance with legal and regulatory requirements 
• Platform analytics and improvement 
• Government reporting and policy analysis 
• Fraud detection and prevention
