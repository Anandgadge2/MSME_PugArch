POLICY 
JSG SMILE – Jharsuguda Supplier & MSME Integrated Local Exchange 
Website: www.jsgsmile.in 
Effective Date: [DD/MM/YYYY] 
 
1. PURPOSE 
This Order Placement & Procurement Facilitation Policy (“Policy”) establishes the 
procedures, responsibilities, and guidelines governing the placement, acceptance, 
execution, tracking, and completion of procurement transactions through JSG SMILE 
(www.jsgsmile.in). 
The objective of this Policy is to facilitate transparent, efficient, and accountable 
procurement transactions between registered Buyers and Suppliers/MSMEs operating 
within Jharsuguda District. 
 
2. NATURE OF THE PORTAL 
2.1 JSG SMILE is a procurement facilitation platform. 
2.2 The Portal enables: 
• Product and service discovery. 
• Requirement posting. 
• RFQ generation. 
• Quotation submission. 
• Purchase order creation. 
• Transaction tracking. 
• Payment tracking. 
• Supplier-Buyer communication. 
2.3 The Portal does not: 
• Act as Buyer. 
• Act as Supplier. 
• Enter into commercial contracts. 
• Negotiate pricing. 
• Guarantee order fulfillment. 
• Guarantee delivery. 
• Guarantee payments. 
 
3. ELIGIBILITY FOR ORDER PLACEMENT 
Orders may be placed only by: 
• Verified Buyers registered on the Portal. 
• Authorized representatives of registered organizations. 
• Users possessing valid procurement authority within their organizations. 
The Portal may request additional authorization documents before permitting order 
placement. 
 
4. PROCUREMENT WORKFLOW 
The procurement process on JSG SMILE may consist of the following stages: 
Stage 1: Requirement Identification 
Buyer identifies procurement requirement. 
Stage 2: Supplier Discovery 
Buyer identifies suitable Suppliers through: 
• Portal search. 
• Product listings. 
• Category browsing. 
• Supplier profiles. 
Stage 3: Request for Quotation (RFQ)  
Buyer may issue RFQ containing: 
• Product specifications. 
• Service requirements. 
• Quantity. 
• Delivery location. 
• Delivery timeline. 
• Commercial terms. 
Stage 4: Quotation Submission 
Suppliers submit quotations containing: 
• Pricing. 
• Delivery schedule. 
• Technical compliance. 
• Validity period. 
• Commercial conditions. 
Stage 5: Evaluation 
Buyer independently evaluates quotations. 
Stage 6: Order Placement 
Buyer places order through the Portal. 
Stage 7: Order Acceptance 
Supplier accepts or declines the order. 
Stage 8: Delivery 
Supplier delivers products/services. 
Stage 9: Confirmation 
Buyer confirms delivery and acceptance. 
Stage 10: Settlement 
Payment settlement is initiated in accordance with applicable policies. 
 
5. ORDER CREATION 
An order may include: 
• Order Number 
• Buyer Information 
• Supplier Information 
• Product/Service Details 
• Quantity 
• Unit Price 
• Total Value 
• Taxes 
• Delivery Terms 
• Payment Terms 
• Special Conditions 
Orders shall be generated electronically through the Portal. 
 
6. ORDER ACCEPTANCE 
6.1 Suppliers shall review all order details before acceptance. 
6.2 Acceptance of an order constitutes the Supplier’s commitment to fulfill the order 
according to agreed terms. 
6.3 Suppliers may reject orders due to: 
• Capacity limitations. 
• Product unavailability. 
• Regulatory restrictions. 
• Commercial reasons. 
6.4 Rejection of an order does not create liability for the Portal. 
 
7. COMMERCIAL TERMS 
Commercial terms including: 
• Pricing 
• Discounts 
• Taxes 
• Delivery charges 
• Warranty conditions 
• Service obligations 
shall be determined solely between Buyer and Supplier. 
The Portal shall not determine commercial terms. 
 
8. DELIVERY REQUIREMENTS 
The Supplier shall: 
• Deliver within agreed timelines. 
• Meet agreed specifications. 
• Provide required documentation. 
• Comply with applicable regulations. 
The Buyer shall: 
• Facilitate delivery acceptance. 
• Conduct inspections where necessary. 
• Provide timely acceptance or rejection. 
 
9. DELIVERY CONFIRMATION 
The Portal may support delivery confirmation through: 
• Delivery Acknowledgement. 
• Goods Receipt Confirmation. 
• Service Completion Confirmation. 
• Digital Acceptance Records. 
• Electronic Sign-Off. 
Such confirmations may be used for transaction tracking and settlement purposes. 
 
10. ORDER MODIFICATION 
Orders may be modified only upon mutual agreement between Buyer and Supplier. 
Modifications may include: 
• Quantity changes. 
• Delivery schedule revisions. 
• Scope changes. 
• Commercial adjustments. 
All modifications shall be electronically recorded where possible. 
 
11. ORDER CANCELLATION 
Orders may be cancelled: 
• By mutual agreement. 
• Due to non-performance. 
• Due to force majeure events. 
• Under applicable commercial agreements. 
Cancellation consequences shall be governed by Buyer-Supplier agreements. 
The Portal shall not determine cancellation penalties. 
 
12. PAYMENT FACILITATION 
Where enabled, JSG SMILE may provide integrated payment processing facilities through 
authorized payment partners. 
The Portal may: 
• Track transaction status. 
• Generate payment references. 
• Record payment milestones. 
• Facilitate settlement workflows. 
The Portal does not act as a bank or financial institution. 
 
13. T+1 SETTLEMENT FRAMEWORK 
Subject to successful completion of transaction milestones: 
1. Buyer confirms delivery or service completion. 
2. Payment status is verified. 
3. Settlement request is initiated. 
4. Settlement may be processed on a T+1 basis. 
Settlement timelines may vary due to: 
• Bank holidays. 
• Payment gateway processing. 
• Compliance reviews. 
• Technical issues. 
• Regulatory requirements. 
The Portal does not guarantee settlement timelines. 
 
14. DISPUTED ORDERS 
Disputes may include: 
• Quality disputes. 
• Quantity disputes. 
• Delivery disputes. 
• Payment disputes. 
• Warranty disputes. 
Such disputes shall be resolved directly between Buyer and Supplier. 
The Portal may facilitate communication but shall not adjudicate disputes. 
 
15. AUDIT TRAIL 
The Portal may maintain records of: 
• RFQs 
• Quotations 
• Orders 
• Delivery confirmations 
• Payment references 
• User actions 
Such records may be used for compliance, reporting, and audit purposes. 
 
16. FRAUD PREVENTION 
The Portal reserves the right to: 
• Review suspicious orders. 
• Request additional documentation. 
• Suspend transactions. 
• Restrict account access. 
• Report unlawful activities to competent authorities. 
 
17. NON-GUARANTEE OF ORDERS 
The Portal does not guarantee: 
• Receipt of quotations. 
• Order awards. 
• Supplier participation. 
• Buyer participation. 
• Business opportunities. 
• Revenue generation. 
All procurement decisions remain solely with participating Buyers and Suppliers.

The Portal and associated stakeholders shall not be liable for:

• Supplier defaults. 
• Buyer defaults. 
• Product defects.
